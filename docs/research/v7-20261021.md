## What changed / 本轮进展

A0 reuses the frozen V6 market-lag control. B1 adds eight causal cost-state inputs based on cached turnover and a 60-session survival-weighted price proxy. B2 reuses B1 forecasts, restricting selection to available cost state, CGO > 0 and five-session market-excess return < 0. This is a truncated engineering proxy, not observed investor holdings or a replication of the papers. No sign flip, threshold rescue or replacement stocks were applied after outcomes.

A0 复用冻结的 V6 对照；B1 新增 8 项换手率与 60 日存活权重成本状态输入；B2 复用预测，仅选成本状态可用、浮盈代理为正、近 5 日市场超额为负的股票。该成本代理不等于真实投资者持仓成本。

The bounded study used 21 new fits, 43 targeted synthetic tests, 126 common mature signal dates (2026-04-01 to 2026-10-08), seven monthly folds, all three horizons and two entry lags. There were no new downloads or old-model refits. Hash checks preserved 1,189 existing files, including 34 original sources and the immutable 50-row forward batch.

## Results and units / 结果与口径

B1 improved observed net returns versus A0 at T+3/T+5 but worsened T+1. B2 reduced observed losses versus B1 in all six groups. Both still had negative observed net returns, negative full-universe rank IC and MSE worse than the historical-mean baseline M0. Relative improvement is not a passed model or stable superiority.

**Return definition:** average across dates of the equal-weight mean of actually selected names with observed outcomes, minus 31 bp round-trip friction. Missing labels are omitted from that observed mean but retained in stress gates. These are holding-period research averages, not cumulative NAV, verified fills, or a fully invested 20-stock portfolio. MSE improvement = 1 − model MSE / M0 MSE; negative means worse.

## Coverage and failed stress checks / 覆盖缺口

B2 selected only 14, 4, 3, 6, 6 and 11 names on 2026-07-13/17/20/21/22/24 respectively: 76 missing stock slots per horizon/lag group. No outside-screen substitutes were added and no dates were dropped. Incomplete Top20 dates leave stress contrasts unavailable, so complete-series and all-bootstrap-replicate gates fail. B1 had no selection-count shortfall, but missing outcome labels remain disclosed in the JSON.

B2 六个日期不足 20 只，每组共缺 76 个股票位置。完整序列和全部 bootstrap 重采样检验未通过；不能把观察收益改善表述为稳定跑赢。

The predeclared bootstrap used 200,000 shared 20-session date-block resamples and a connected family of 510 tests. Adjusted tail precision is limited; this is development screening, not confirmatory significance.

## Prior failure diagnosis and boundaries / 前序诊断与边界

Read-only diagnosis of saved V6 outputs covered all seven months: the peer-feature arm had positive IC in every horizon/lag group in May and June, then negative IC in all six in July. October contains only one mature date. This establishes instability, not permission to delete July, reverse scores or rerun the old study. The underlying 112 diagnostic inputs remained unchanged.

These historical dates have already been examined. V7 remains RESEARCH_ONLY / REUSED_DEVELOPMENT_COMPARISON; it does not supply new prospective evidence. Website replacement still requires at least 60 newly matured prospective dates over three months and the required independent review. The present checks are collaborative implementation verification, not an independent audit. No Sites redeployment or model promotion is part of this research publication.
