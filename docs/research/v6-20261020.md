# V6 paper-feature and selection study / 论文特征与选股验证

Completed 20 October 2026. Two candidate policies failed all six development groups. The public V1 model and existing daily forecast/outcome records are retained.

Implemented five-lag peer/idiosyncratic return decomposition and a fixed concentration selection screen. A0 is the architecture control, A1 the feature candidate, and A2 reuses A1 forecasts with a different eligible pool. All three horizons and both entry clocks were evaluated with 42 new Ridge fits and 31 passing synthetic tests.

本轮落实五日滞后收益的同类股票/个股分解，以及固定的集中度筛选。
A0 为未拆分的架构对照；A1 为拆分特征候选；A2 复用 A1 预测并筛选集中度。

范围：20260401–20261008，126 个共同成熟交易日，7 个月份折；42 次新拟合。
全 A 股原合格股票范围，T+1/T+3/T+5，各验证两种入场时钟。成本情景为往返31bp。

A1's peer split worsened net returns in five of six groups versus A0. A2 improved net returns in all six groups versus A1, but remained negative and below its same-pool market, momentum and low-volatility references. Every candidate group had negative daily rank IC and worse MSE than the training-mean baseline. These are reused development data; no independent prospective success or model promotion is claimed.

## Primary papers / 主要文献

- [Nagel (2012), Evaporating Liquidity](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653)
- [Da, Liu and Schaumburg (2014), A Closer Look at the Short-Term Return Reversal](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2013.1766)
- [Neszveda et al. (2022), daily reversal in China](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2022.103220) — publisher abstract evidence.

The papers motivate mechanisms; their markets, horizons and execution assumptions differ from this daily A-share long-only Top20 study. No paper text or private raw dataset is redistributed here.
