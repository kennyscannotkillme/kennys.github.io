## Methods actually tested / 实际完成的方法

- **A0:** exact saved V8 continuous-state Ridge predictions, with no refit.
- **D1:** adds historical market-cap and book-to-market proxies, strict-prior 20-session market-relative return salience, and missingness indicators to the existing Ridge recipe.
- **D2:** estimates future residual returns directly from a fixed size × BM × salience × volatility grid. It averages stock returns within each cell/date, then averages across training dates with fixed support shrinkage. The two entry lags have separate mappings. It is a different method and representation, not only a weight adjustment.

D1 新增已保存的市值、PB 倒数与严格先前窗口的收益显著性特征。D2 改为用四维特征分组，直接估计后续残差收益。V3 已使用分组均值与收缩，因此本轮新意在直接按新特征分组，而非声称首次引入该数学公式。

The complete eligible A-share universe is scored, without limiting candidates to the sectors of personal interest. Top20 uses the unchanged predeclared liquid S pool, ranks predicted return, and is chosen before outcomes are joined. Missing features or weak cell support do not remove names. These are local research adaptations, not exact paper replications.

Completed work: **21 native Ridge fits + 21 table bundles containing 42 lag-specific tables**, seven monthly folds, three horizons and two entry lags. Evaluation uses **126 common mature dates, 1 April–8 October 2026**. There were **0 old-model refits and 0 new market-data downloads**. The targeted suite passed **100 tests** with three dependency deprecation warnings. Preservation checks confirmed **1,994 existing file hashes**, the original **34 source files**, and the immutable **50-row forward batch** unchanged.

## What changed and what did not / 哪些改善没有成立

D1 worsened full-period observed net selection returns versus A0 in five of six groups, and worsened July returns in all six. D2 reduced MSE relative to D1 in all six, but still lost to M0 and produced worse full-period Top20 returns in all six. Its July improvement versus D1 did not generalize to the full period. Lower prediction error within two failed models is not evidence of better stock selection.

Full selection coverage therefore does not imply fine individual-stock discrimination. Deterministic ticker ordering resolved exact ties without adding artificial score differences.

D2 虽然每天选满 20 只，但预测值集中于少量分组，排名边界存在大量并列。数量够用不等于能精确分辨最好的个股。该观察支持保留并列诊断，不能据此事后调整分组或打破并列规则。

## Evidence boundaries and decision / 证据边界与决定

The study uses repeatedly examined development dates and historical vendor vintages, not fresh out-of-sample or archival accounting-PIT evidence. A prior-session PB value does not establish that accounting data were never revised. Book-to-market is a vendor proxy, not the China paper's preferred earnings/price measure. News-provider integration is separate and supplied no historical features to this run.

Uncertainty uses 200,000 shared noncircular 20-session date-block resamples and a connected family of 630 comparisons. The corrected tail has limited numerical resolution, as retained in the JSON. Missing-outcome stress scenarios are explicit assumptions, not guaranteed mathematical bounds.

**Decision: close this bounded V9 study without replacing the public model.** No automatic recipe adjustment, new search, model promotion or rewrite of past forecasts follows. Any eventual replacement still requires at least 60 newly matured prospective dates over three months and the established review requirements. Collaborative implementation checks are not an independent promotion audit.

**本轮 V9 按既定配方结束，不替换网页模型。** 历史预测与实际收益继续保留。开发期验证失败如实归档，不写成已验证盈利能力，也不自动修改失败配方再试。

## Primary papers / 原始文献

- [Cosemans and Frehen (2021), Salience Theory and Stock Prices](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.12.012): author 2017 full-text methodology inspected. Monthly US evidence motivates return-state salience; the strict-prior daily window is a local adaptation.
- [Liu, Stambaugh and Yuan (2019), Size and value in China](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.03.008): author institutional manuscript inspected. Preferred value variable is EP; the paper's smallest-30% exclusion was not adopted.
- [Cattaneo et al. (2020), Characteristic-Sorted Portfolios](https://doi.org/10.1162/rest_a_00883): published author copy inspected. Supports the estimator class and documents dimensionality concerns, not this grid's profitability.
