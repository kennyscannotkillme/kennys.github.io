## Methods actually tested / 已完成的方法

A0 reuses frozen V7 B1 without refitting. C1 adds eight strict-prior 60-pair overnight/intraday market-demeaned dependence and current-signal interactions, with a missingness indicator. C2 adds four continuous stock/market-state interactions and its indicator. Phase-return levels already existed in V3; the new hypothesis is dependence and state interaction, not renaming old return levels. These are engineering adaptations of research mechanisms, not paper replications.

Both candidates rank the complete original S pool by predicted return and take its first 20 names. The rare V7 CGO/relative-loss conjunction was removed before outcomes; there is no sector restriction, outcome-driven replacement or fallback. The full eligible A-share universe still receives predictions.

Completed: 42 new native fits, 48 targeted tests, 126 common mature dates from 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-08, seven monthly folds, T+1/T+3/T+5 with both entry lags. No new downloads or old-model refits. Preservation checks cover 1,375 existing file hashes, the original 34 sources and immutable 50-row forward batch.

## Coverage improved; prediction did not pass / 覆盖改善，预测未通过

C1 and C2 each selected 20 names on every one of 126 dates in all six horizon/lag groups: zero selection shortfall. Missing realized outcomes remain missing and are retained in stress tests. Selecting 20 names does not mean that all 20 realized returns are available or that trades were executable.

C2 improved July observed net cohort means versus A0 by 0.31–0.54 percentage points across the six groups, but July IC remained negative in all six. Full-period IC and net cohort means are also negative, MSE is worse than M0, and neither candidate passes stability/economic gates. July improvement is not stable profitability or a reason to replace V1.

## Results and units / 结果与口径

Overlapping cohorts are not compounded account NAV, broker returns or verified execution. Market excess is the percentage-point difference; MSE improvement = 1 − model MSE / M0 MSE, so negative means worse.

## Evidence boundaries / 证据边界

The bootstrap uses 200,000 shared noncircular 20-session date-block resamples with a connected family of 570 tests. All previously failed criteria and missing-result counts remain in the aggregate JSON. The July relationship change is an observed association, not proof of a macroeconomic cause.

These are repeatedly examined development dates, not fresh OOS or prospective evidence. Status remains RESEARCH_ONLY / REUSED_DEVELOPMENT_COMPARISON. Historical improvement never automatically replaces the website: at least 60 newly matured prospective dates over three months and the required independent review remain necessary. Collaborative checks are not an independent model audit.
